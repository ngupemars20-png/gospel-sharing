ABOUT THIS PROJECT

About Catholic Gospel Sharing

Created and developed by

NGUBE MARCELLUS NGUPE

A Catholic initiative designed to support prayerful reflection and meaningful sharing of the Sunday Gospel.

This app brings together the Seven Steps of Gospel Sharing, a Self-Evaluation Scheme and Sunday Scripture references in one accessible guide.

For further enquiries, contact the app designer at ngupemars20@gmail.com.

The app is intended as a guide for personal and group Gospel sharing.
