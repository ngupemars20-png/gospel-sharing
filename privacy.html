Privacy Policy

GOSPEL SHARING

Effective date: 26 November 2026

1. Introduction

GOSPEL SHARING is a Catholic application developed and published by MARCO'S IDEAS. It provides resources for Sunday Gospel reflection, prayer, Bible sharing and Christian spiritual growth.

We respect the privacy of our users and aim to handle information responsibly and transparently.

2. Personal Information

The application does not require users to register an account or provide their names, telephone numbers or personal addresses to access its main Bible-sharing resources.

3. Usage Statistics

Certain versions of the application may record basic, anonymous readership statistics, such as the number of times reflection content is viewed. These statistics help us understand how the application is being used and improve its content.

We do not intend to use these statistics to identify individual users. Where technical identifiers are used to estimate unique devices, they are not treated as verified personal identities.

4. Internet Connection and Third-Party Services

Some features may require an internet connection. Where the application communicates with external services, those services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

GOSPEL SHARING is intended as a Christian educational and spiritual resource. We do not knowingly require children to submit personal information to use its main features.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its information practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
